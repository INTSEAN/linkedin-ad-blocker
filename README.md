# **LinkedIn Ad Blocker**  

A lightweight and efficient Chrome extension built with JavaScript to block ads on LinkedIn.

## **Installation Guide**  

Follow these simple steps to install the extension in Chrome:  

### **1. Open Chrome Settings**  

- Click on the **three-dot menu** in the top-right corner of your Chrome window.  
- Select **"Settings"** from the dropdown menu.  

### **2. Access Extensions**  

- In the left sidebar, scroll down and click **"Extensions"** to open the Extensions Manager.  

### **3. Enable Developer Mode**  

- Locate the **"Developer mode"** toggle in the top-right corner and turn it on.  

### **4. Load the Extension**  

- Click **"Load Unpacked"** and select your project’s directory.  

✅ **That's it!** Your LinkedIn Ad Blocker is now active and ready to enhance your browsing experience.
